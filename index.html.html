Islamic Companion , Privacy Policy

The short version: Islamic Companion runs entirely on your device. It collects no data, shares no data, contains no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers, and does not even request internet access. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device unless you yourself export it.

Who we are

Islamic Companion is an independent prayer-times, Quran, and worship companion app for Android, published by Haris (“we” or “the developer”). Contact: harisnadeem98876@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no server, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash-reporting libraries. Its Android release build does not request the internet permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting your information anywhere.

Information that stays on your device

To do its job, the app stores some information locally on your device only, in the app’s private storage:

  • Location (optional): if you choose “Use my location”, the app reads your device’s GPS position once to compute accurate prayer times and qibla direction on the device itself. The coordinates are saved locally so times keep working offline. If you prefer, you can instead pick a city from the built-in offline list and the app never touches GPS. Location is never transmitted: there is nowhere for it to go.
  • Your settings and worship records: prayer calculation preferences, notification choices, bookmarks and notes, memorization (hifz) progress, tasbeeh counts, prayer and fasting tracker entries, and custom calendar events.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Backups are yours

The optional backup feature exports your data as a single readable file that you choose where to save (for example your Downloads folder or your own cloud drive). The app never uploads it. Restoring simply reads a file you select. What you do with your backup file, and where you store it, is entirely under your control.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Location (fine/coarse): optional; a one-time read to compute prayer times and qibla on-device, as described above.
  • Notifications: to show prayer-time alerts and the optional morning/evening adhkar reminders you configure.
  • Exact alarms: prayer times are time-critical, so the app asks Android to deliver its alerts at the exact minute rather than whenever the system finds convenient.
  • Run at startup: to re-schedule your prayer alerts after the phone reboots, so no alert is missed.
  • Vibration: for the tasbeeh counter’s haptic feedback.

The app does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, storage-wide, or any other permission.

Wear OS companion

If you use the optional watch app, prayer times and tasbeeh counts sync directly between your phone and watch over the local Bluetooth/Wi-Fi link provided by the operating system. This is device-to-device communication; no internet service is involved and the developer receives nothing.

Third-party services

None. The app embeds no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no social SDKs, and makes no network calls. Prayer times, the hijri calendar, qibla, the full Quran text with translations, and the supplication collections are all computed from, or bundled with, the app itself.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights over personal data that organisations hold about you. We hold none: there is no account to delete and no database containing you. Your data lives on your device, where you can export it (backup) or erase it (clear the app’s data or uninstall) at any time without asking anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes these practices, for example an optional online feature, this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the change will be described plainly. The commitment that core worship features work fully offline will not change.
