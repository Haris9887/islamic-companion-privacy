Islamic Companion · Support

Prayer times, Quran, qibla and more, for Android, iPhone and iPad

Need help or found a problem? Email islamiccompanion.support@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened, and a screenshot if you can. Replies usually come within a few days.

The prayer times don't match my mosque

Open Settings and choose the calculation method your mosque uses (for example Muslim World League, ISNA or Umm al-Qura), and the Asr time (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali or Hanafi). You can also adjust each prayer by a few minutes.

I'm not getting prayer alerts

Check that notifications are allowed for Islamic Companion in your phone's settings, and that the alerts are switched on in the app's Settings. Open the app every few days so the next alerts are scheduled.

The widget says to open the app

Open the app once. It saves the next few days of prayer times for the widgets, which then update on their own.

The qibla compass looks wrong

Move away from metal objects and magnets, then calibrate your phone's compass by moving it in a figure of eight. Some phones and most iPads have no compass, so the app shows the qibla bearing instead.

Moving to a new phone

Use Backup in the app to save a file with your bookmarks, tracker and settings, then restore that file on the new phone.
